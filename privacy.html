Boundary privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Boundary is a cricket scoring and decision review app. This page says what the app does with your information, in plain words.

The short version

  • Your matches, grounds, player names, reviews and settings are saved on your own device.
  • Video from the camera stays on your device. Boundary does not upload it.
  • Boundary has no adverts and does not sell or share your information.

What is stored, and where

On your device only: match scores, team and player names you type in, saved grounds and local rules, review decisions, your theme and other settings. Deleting the app's data, or using Settings, Delete all data, removes all of it.

Camera: Boundary asks for the camera so it can show a live view and keep the last few seconds for replay. That video is held in the device's memory and is discarded when you leave the camera screen. A clip is only saved if you tap Download clip, and it is saved to your own device.

Microphone: Boundary asks for the microphone only when you tap Voice. The speech is turned into text by your browser's or phone's own speech service (on Android with Chrome, that is Google's). Boundary receives only the text, uses it to suggest a score, and does not store the audio.

Services Boundary talks to

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when the app loads.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typefaces. Your device requests them from Google when online.
  • Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication), only if you choose to sign in. Boundary then receives your name, email address and a user ID from Google, used only to recognise your account. Signing in is optional and the app works without it.

Recording other people

If you record a match, you are responsible for asking the people in it first. For players under 18, ask a parent, guardian or the school. Do not share clips of other people without their agreement.

Children

Boundary does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app can be used without an account and without giving any personal details.

Your choices

  • Refuse camera or microphone access: scoring still works.
  • Back up or delete everything from Settings, Data on this phone.
  • Sign out at any time from Settings, Account. To have a sign-in account removed, contact us.

Changes

If the app starts storing anything online, such as cloud match history or payments, this page will be updated before that feature is switched on.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/karibhasan386-coder/boundary/issues.
